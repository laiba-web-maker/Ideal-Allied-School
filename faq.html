Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. When do admissions open?

Admissions are announced by the school administration every year. Please visit the Admissions section or contact the school for the latest updates.

2. What documents are required for admission?

B-Form/Birth Certificate, Father's CNIC Copy, Passport Size Photographs, and Previous School Result Card (if applicable).

3. What are the school timings?

Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM.

4. How can I contact the school?

You can contact us through Call or WhatsApp during school timings.

5. Where is the school located?

The complete school address and Google Map are available in the Contact Us section.
