School Introduction

Ideal Allied High School 97 JB is a well-established educational institution dedicated to providing quality education in a disciplined, safe and friendly environment. Our school focuses on academic excellence, character building and the overall personality development of every student.

We believe that every child has unique potential. Through experienced teachers, modern teaching methods and co-curricular activities, we help our students become confident, responsible and successful individuals.

Our goal is to prepare students not only for examinations but also for real-life challenges by promoting knowledge, discipline, honesty and leadership qualities.

History

Ideal Allied High School 97 JB was established with the vision of providing quality education to the students of the surrounding community. Since its foundation, the school has remained committed to academic excellence, discipline and character building.

Over the years, the school has earned the trust of parents by providing a safe learning environment, experienced teachers and excellent academic results. Our journey continues with the same dedication to preparing students for a bright and successful future.

Vision

Our vision is to become a leading educational institution that provides high-quality education while nurturing confidence, creativity, discipline, Islamic values and leadership qualities in every student. We strive to prepare our students to face future challenges with knowledge, honesty, responsibility and strong moral character.

Rules & Regulations

  • Students must arrive at school on time every day.
  • School uniform is compulsory for all students.
  • Respect teachers, staff and fellow students at all times.
  • Maintain cleanliness in classrooms and on the school campus.
  • Mobile phones and unnecessary electronic devices are not allowed.
  • Students must complete homework and class assignments regularly.
  • Attendance is compulsory according to school rules.
  • Any damage to school property will be the responsibility of the student.
